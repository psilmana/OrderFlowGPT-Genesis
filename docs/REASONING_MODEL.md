# Reasoning Model

The Reasoning Model defines how Genesis should interpret Order Flow lessons.

Genesis should not jump from detection directly to knowledge or prediction. It should reason through Fabio Valentini's playbook hierarchy.

## Fabio Hierarchy

Genesis should reason in this order:

1. Market State.
2. Location.
3. Aggression.
4. Risk.
5. Management.
6. Reflection.

## Reasoning Stages

### 1. Market State

Genesis first identifies the broader market condition.

Questions include:

- Is the market balanced or imbalanced?
- Is it trending, rotating, accepting, or rejecting?
- What auction context matters?
- What session or multi-timeframe context is relevant?

### 2. Location

Genesis then evaluates where the event occurs.

Questions include:

- Is price at a meaningful level?
- Is the location premium, discount, extreme, midpoint, value, or breakout area?
- Does the location support or weaken the idea?

### 3. Aggression

Genesis evaluates buyer and seller behaviour.

Questions include:

- Who is aggressive?
- Is aggression continuing or failing?
- Is there absorption, exhaustion, initiative, or responsive behaviour?
- Does order flow confirm or contradict the context?

### 4. Risk

Genesis evaluates invalidation and exposure.

Questions include:

- Where is the idea wrong?
- Is the risk structurally justified?
- Is the opportunity asymmetric?
- Should the apprentice avoid action because risk is unclear?

### 5. Management

Genesis evaluates how the situation should be managed.

Questions include:

- What would confirm continuation?
- What would require reducing confidence?
- What would invalidate the premise?
- How should new evidence update the interpretation?

### 6. Reflection

Genesis reflects after the lesson or outcome.

Questions include:

- What did Genesis understand correctly?
- What did Genesis miss?
- What did Fabio explain differently?
- What should be remembered for future similar cases?

## Explanation Before Prediction

Genesis should explain the reasoning hierarchy before offering any predictive interpretation. Prediction without explanation is outside the GLA v1 learning posture.

## Knowledge Objects

Knowledge objects should be indexed by this hierarchy. A knowledge object should clarify which reasoning stage it supports and which experiences produced it.
