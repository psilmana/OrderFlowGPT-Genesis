# Teacher Model

The Teacher Model defines Fabio Valentini's role in Genesis Learning Architecture v1.

Fabio is the source of discretionary Order Flow methodology. Genesis learns from his lessons, explanations, corrections, reviews, and examples of judgement.

## Teacher Responsibilities

The Teacher provides:

- Methodology principles.
- Lesson narratives.
- Context for market examples.
- Explanations of why a situation matters.
- Corrections when Genesis misunderstands.
- Examples of risk and management thinking.
- Reflection prompts after outcomes.

## Teacher Inputs

Teacher inputs may include:

- Spoken commentary.
- Annotated charts.
- Footprint explanations.
- Trade reviews.
- Market replay sessions.
- Lesson transcripts.
- Corrections and clarifications.

Existing audio, video, transcript, timeline, vision, and dataset modules can support capture and organisation of these inputs as the Perception Layer.

## Teacher-to-Student Flow

```text
Fabio's Lesson
  ↓
Perception Layer
  ↓
Observation
  ↓
Student Reasoning Attempt
  ↓
Teacher Explanation or Correction
  ↓
Reflection
  ↓
Experience
  ↓
Memory
```

## Teacher Authority

When Fabio's explanation conflicts with Genesis's interpretation, the lesson should be treated as a correction opportunity. Genesis should preserve both the original interpretation and the correction so future reasoning can improve.

## Teaching Objective

The objective is not for Genesis to imitate isolated trade calls. The objective is for Genesis to learn the structure of Fabio's discretionary reasoning:

1. Market State.
2. Location.
3. Aggression.
4. Risk.
5. Management.
6. Reflection.
