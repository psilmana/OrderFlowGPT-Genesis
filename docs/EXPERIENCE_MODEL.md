# Experience Model

The Experience Model is the centre of Genesis Learning Architecture v1.

Genesis no longer revolves around frames as the primary learning unit. It revolves around Experiences.

## From Frames to Experiences

### Old Model

```text
Frame
  ↓
Detection
  ↓
Knowledge
```

### New Model

```text
Observation
  ↓
Reasoning
  ↓
Decision
  ↓
Outcome
  ↓
Reflection
  ↓
Experience
```

## Experience Definition

An Experience is a complete learning episode that connects what Genesis perceived, how it reasoned, what decision or judgement was considered, what happened afterward, and what reflection changed in memory.

## Experience Components

| Component | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Observation | Evidence perceived from vision, video, transcript, audio, timeline, or dataset sources. |
| Reasoning | Structured interpretation using Fabio's hierarchy. |
| Decision | The bias, action, non-action, or judgement discussed in the lesson. |
| Outcome | What followed in the market or lesson review. |
| Reflection | What Genesis learned, misunderstood, corrected, or should remember. |

## Experience Lifecycle

1. Capture lesson evidence through the Perception Layer.
2. Convert evidence into observations.
3. Reason using Fabio's hierarchy.
4. Attach the relevant decision or discretionary judgement.
5. Record the outcome.
6. Reflect on the gap between expectation, teacher explanation, and result.
7. Store the complete Experience in Memory.
8. Allow repeated Experiences to form Knowledge.

## Experience and Knowledge

Knowledge should emerge from multiple Experiences. A single observation or detection is not enough to become durable knowledge. Durable knowledge should be supported by context, reasoning, outcomes, and reflection.
